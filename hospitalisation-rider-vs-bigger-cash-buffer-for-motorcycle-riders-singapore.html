Hospitalisation Rider vs Cash Buffer for Riders Singapore: Keep the Deductible

An Integrated Shield Plan rider can reduce eligible medical co-payment. Accessible cash still funds the deductible, uncovered treatment, household needs and payment timing. Compare the actual main plan and rider with your preferred care setting and a dated cash ledger, rather than assuming either a rider or a reserve covers the whole recovery period.

Check the issue date and rider version

MOH's requirements for new IP riders sold from 1 April 2026 leave the minimum IP deductible uncovered. Co-payment remains at least 5%, with the permitted annual co-payment cap at least S$6,000, excluding that deductible. Caps apply to eligible claims, such as panel or pre-authorised care, under the policy conditions. See MOH's design requirements and transition rules.

Non-compliant riders purchased from 27 November 2025 to 31 March 2026 must transition no later than the next renewal after 1 April 2028. Earlier contracts depend on the insurer's approach; do not assume all existing riders changed on 1 April 2026. Check your issued schedule and renewal notice.

A rider is an optional addition to a main IP. It is not a replacement for the main medical plan. Confirm the deductible, policy year, hospital/ward scope, panel and pre-authorisation terms, exclusions, claim limits and actual co-payment cap before modelling a bill. A S$6,000 co-payment cap is not a cap on every household expense.

Calculate deductible and co-payment separately

This selected comparison assumes a S$40,000 eligible private-hospital bill within the main plan's limits, a S$3,500 annual deductible not yet used, and no previous claims that policy year. The main-plan-only case has 10% co-insurance without an additional cap in this model. The new-compliant selected rider case has 5% co-payment, a S$6,000 eligible annual cap excluding the deductible, and all conditions for that cap met. MediSave can meet eligible medical charges subject to balances and withdrawal limits. It does not turn into unrestricted money for rent, loan payments or backup travel. Separate gross liability, any permitted withdrawal and required cash; do not assume an entire deductible or cap is automatically payable from MediSave.

Test a large bill and claims outside the selected cap

For a single eligible S$150,000 bill under the same selected terms and unused annual deductible/cap, S$146,500 remains after deductible. Five percent is S$7,325; the selected S$6,000 cap reduces that co-payment to S$6,000. Add the retained S$3,500 deductible: medical liability is S$9,500. Main-plan-only 10% co-insurance is S$14,650, giving liability S$18,150 in this model.

Adding the same S$3,792.50 household and uncovered items gives S$13,292.50 with the selected rider, still S$1,292.50 above the S$12,000 event envelope. If the claim fails the selected cap conditions and, purely as a stress assumption, 5% co-payment remains uncapped, liability would instead be S$10,825 and full event cash S$14,617.50. Actual non-panel or non-authorised terms may differ more substantially; obtain them rather than assuming this stress formula is a contractual fallback.

Multiple bills, policy-year changes, pro-ration, exclusions and benefits already used require a new ledger. Apply annual deductible and cap balances under the contract, rather than repeatedly charging a full unused deductible or assigning a new cap to every bill.

FAQ

Does a new 2026 IP rider remove the deductible?

Under the new rider requirements, the minimum IP deductible remains payable separately. Check the actual rider version and issued terms; a co-payment cap does not include that retained deductible.

Is S$6,000 the most my household can need?

No. The selected co-payment cap excludes the deductible and applies only to eligible claims under its conditions. Uncovered treatment, deposits, income needs and transport costs require separate funding.

Did every existing rider change on 1 April 2026?

No blanket change to every older contract should be assumed. Check purchase date, the insurer's transition approach and the issued renewal terms.

Can MediSave replace accessible emergency cash?

MediSave use is restricted to eligible charges and withdrawal limits. Confirm its permitted contribution to medical payments, and keep unrestricted household and transport cash separate.
