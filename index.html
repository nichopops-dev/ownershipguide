Understand the assumptions behind the answer

Our calculators are planning models. A useful result shows what is included, what is left out, and which assumptions could change the decision. Try your expected case, then a lower income or higher cost.

For rates, eligibility, and transaction rules, follow the official sources linked in each guide. A calculator result is not a bank offer, insurance quotation, or care assessment.

Common questions

Do I have to read a guide before using a calculator?

No. If you know your inputs, open the calculator directly. Read its assumptions and use the linked guide when you need help interpreting the result.

Does this site cover Singapore rules?

Yes. The guides focus on Singapore household decisions, including COE, CPF, property financing, family care, and insurance. Check the linked official sources before acting on a rule or rate.

How should I use a result?

Use it to compare scenarios and identify the costs that matter most. Replace example inputs with your own quotations and check whether the decision still works under less favourable assumptions.
