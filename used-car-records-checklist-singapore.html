Used-Car Records: Reconcile Dates, Mileage, Identity and Work

Records do not prove a car is trouble-free. They let you test whether the seller’s story, official vehicle details, odometer history and physical condition agree. Build one timeline and mark every missing or contradictory item.

Verify official details before interpreting invoices

Obtain the owner’s access code and use LTA’s transfer-fee enquiry to check registration details, age, COE expiry and PARF eligibility. Tally the physical engine and chassis identifiers with LTA records before accepting transfer.

What each record can and cannot establish

Use records together; no single document closes the diligence process
RecordUseful evidenceWhat it does not prove
LTA registration detailsRegistered specifications, age, COE expiry, PARF eligibility and identifiers.Mechanical condition or complete accident history.
Service invoicesDated mileage, workshop, described work, parts and recurring patterns.That omitted work never occurred or that every diagnosis was correct.
Periodic inspection resultThe listed systems passed the statutory inspection at that time.A complete pre-purchase mechanical assessment or future reliability.

Warranty documentContractual duration, covered components, exclusions and claim process.That a future claim will qualify or eliminate downtime.
Seller statementA written representation that can be compared with other evidence.Truth by itself. Verify material claims independently.

LTA advises buyers to verify registration details through the official service, physically tally vehicle identifiers and check that markings are visible and untampered. Build a mileage and work timeline

Record date, odometer, workshop, work and invoice identifier. For example: 42,100 km in January 2024; 55,800 km in December 2024; 61,400 km in August 2025; and 78,900 km at the September 2026 viewing. The increase is 36,800 km over about 32 months, or roughly 13,800 km a year. That is internally coherent, but it is not proof that no reading or invoice is missing.

A March 2026 invoice showing 84,200 km followed by a current 79,000 km reading is a material contradiction. Ask for a documented explanation and verify independently. Do not replace the recorded number with a more convenient assumption.

Look for patterns, not just volume

  • Repeated work on the same system can indicate an unresolved cause, even when the file is thick.
  • A long gap weakens confidence but does not prove neglect; ask where the vehicle was serviced.
  • Recent major work may reduce near-term exposure or reveal a recurring failure. Read the diagnosis and warranty on the repair.
  • An “accident-free” claim is not established by the absence of an accident invoice.
  • Modification invoices should be checked against current LTA requirements and the physical car.

Car B is S$2,500 cheaper and still S$1,300 cheaper after known immediate work. Once the separate reserve for unresolved uncertainty is considered, it needs S$700 more liquidity. This does not assign a universal price to missing records. It shows how records, inspection findings and cash resilience must be considered together.

Do not use a contingency twice: if a later diagnosis turns part of the reserve into a specific S$1,500 repair, reduce the reserve assumption or clearly explain why additional uncertainty remains.

Build the final handover file

  • Official registration details and transfer-fee enquiry used for the decision.
  • Signed sale agreement, itemised price, deposit terms and all written representations.
  • Independent evaluation, seller disclosure checklist and repair commitments.
  • Service invoices, current odometer photograph, keys and accessory inventory.
  • Financing approval or settlement confirmation, insurance and payment receipts.
  • Ownership-transfer confirmation and the final vehicle details accepted in OneMotoring.

LTA says any verbal dealer promises should be put in the agreement, payments should go to the company UEN, and official receipts should be retained. LTA transaction safeguards. Keep the file after transfer in case a contractual or consumer issue arises.

Frequently asked questions

Do complete service records prove a used car is reliable?

No. They improve traceability but do not reveal every hidden or intermittent defect. Reconcile them with official details and an independent evaluation.

How should I handle a mileage contradiction?

Pause the purchase, ask for a documented explanation and verify independently. Do not assume the lower or more convenient number is correct.

Is an uncertainty reserve part of the car’s cost?

It is a liquidity requirement until spent. Keep it separate from quoted repairs and record actual spending later so the same risk is not counted twice.
