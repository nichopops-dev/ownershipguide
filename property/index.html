Keep four budget questions separate

Passing an eligibility or loan check answers only part of the decision
QuestionWhat to include
Can I buy this property?Eligibility, ownership restrictions, applicable grants and the chosen property’s conditions
Can I complete the purchase?Cash and usable CPF available by each deadline; loan; duties; fees; valuation gap; sale-proceeds timing
Can I carry the home?Mortgage instalments, taxes, maintenance, insurance, repairs and other household commitments
What will ownership cost?Interest, transaction and running costs, and change in property value over the chosen hold; avoid treating repaid principal as a separate economic loss

For illustration, S$7,500 take-home income minus S$3,000 household spending, a S$2,200 mortgage, S$500 property running costs and S$800 planned savings leaves S$1,000 a month. A 20% income reduction changes that to a S$500 shortfall. These are hypothetical budgets; replace them with actual costs and test the mortgage rate separately.

Use TDSR and MSR for lending constraints and mortgage amortisation for the repayment schedule. Personal affordability also depends on childcare, eldercare and income stability.

A practical sequence for an HDB resale purchase

  1. Eligibility: obtain the HFE letter and check its expiry.
  2. Budget: establish loan and CPF availability, then set a cash limit including a possible valuation gap.
  3. Property and contract: inspect the flat, agree the price, and follow the OTP and Request for Value process.
  4. Completion and move: reconcile the payment schedule, renovation and reserve before treating the home as affordable.

The HFE and COV guides give the current HDB process details. A private-property or developer-sale purchase follows its own procedure; use the relevant route and your solicitor’s instructions.

When another household decision changes the home plan

Check buying with parents or family before committing jointly. If care needs are changing, use the aging-parent support guide alongside location and cash planning. The Family hub covers childcare and caregiving costs that compete for the same income.

Owners comparing a move with staying should model net sale proceeds and replacement costs together. A higher selling price does not by itself establish how much cash remains after the loan, CPF refund and next purchase.
