Full-Day Childcare vs Half-Day Preschool: Fees and Care Hours

Choose the programme that covers the hours your household actually needs. Half-day preschool can cost less when a willing adult reliably covers the rest of the day. Once you add paid afternoon care, extra travel or reduced earnings, the cheaper school fee may produce a higher household cost.

Quick answer

Compare full-day childcare, half-day preschool plus home care, and MOE Kindergarten plus KCare as three different arrangements. Price the complete weekday, school holidays and backup plan. Compare programmes for the same eligible child and start date; an infant-care place and an older child’s kindergarten place are not substitutes.

Do not apply the childcare subsidy to a kindergarten invoice. Kindergarten Fee Assistance Scheme (KiFAS) support and KCare assistance are separate. Confirm your child’s approved net amounts, especially for part-day programmes, a changed working arrangement or a 2027 start date.

In this illustration, the family-care option saves S$164.90 a month against full-day childcare. If the household instead pays S$250 monthly for the afternoon care, its total rises to S$590, which is S$85.10 more than the full-day example.

The cash break-even for that afternoon-care payment is S$164.90 a month, holding every other assumption fixed. That does not price a relative’s effort. Four hours of afternoon care on 20 weekdays is 80 hours of care. Record those hours and the caregiver’s willingness independently; do not label unpaid time free or turn an assumed hourly value into a bill that nobody pays.

If a parent must reduce paid work, calculate the actual change in take-home income separately. Avoid adding both foregone pay and an imputed value for the same hours to one cash total. Review preschool versus a stay-at-home parent and childcare versus grandparent care for those wider decisions.

The Tampines MK fee schedule also spreads school fees over 12 months, including June and December. Paying the school fee in a holiday month does not itself buy holiday supervision. Full-day childcare also needs a backup for closures or days the child cannot attend.

Which arrangement fits your household?

  • Two adults with fixed work hours: compare full-day childcare with MK plus KCare using net fees, travel and the actual collection deadline.
  • A parent intentionally works part-time: half-day preschool can fit if the afternoon care aligns with that chosen work pattern.
  • A willing nearby grandparent: agree days, travel, rest and a backup. Reassess if the arrangement depends on recurring overtime from the caregiver.
  • A helper already provides afternoon care: compare the additional school and travel cost, while keeping the helper’s whole employment cost visible in the wider household budget.

Visit the shortlisted programmes and assess the child’s routine, interactions, environment and your communication with educators. Programme length alone does not establish educational quality or suitability.

Questions to put to each centre

  1. What are the exact daily hours, holiday dates and late-collection terms?
  2. What is the written fee after GST and the child’s approved subsidies?
  3. Which deposits, registration fees and extras are separate, and what is refundable?
  4. Is the needed care place confirmed for the intended start date?
  5. What happens to fees and subsidies if the child changes programme or the family withdraws?

Frequently asked questions

Is half-day preschool always cheaper?

No. Add afternoon care, travel, backup arrangements and any actual loss of take-home pay. A smaller school bill does not establish a lower household cost.

Can MOE Kindergarten provide a full-day arrangement?

MOE Kindergarten can be combined with its separate KCare service. Confirm the care place, hours, calendar and net fee with the selected centre.

Should I deduct S$300 childcare subsidy from a kindergarten fee?

No. The subsidy schemes differ. Use the approved kindergarten assistance and KCare support for their respective invoices.

How should I account for unpaid family care?

Record the hours, caregiver agreement and backup plan separately from cash spending. Add actual payments or reduced take-home income only where they occur.
